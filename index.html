        🍪 Privacy & Cookies
        This app runs entirely on localhost — your data never leaves your machine. We only store your login token and job applications locally. Read privacy policy →

        🔒 Privacy Policy — CyberCareer

        What is stored

        • Login token — in LocalStorage, expires after 24h
        • Hashed password — in storage.json on your PC (scrypt)
        • Applications — title, company, status — local only
        • CV text — RAM only, never saved to disk

        External API calls

        When searching jobs, your IP is sent to: Bundesagentur für Arbeit, Arbeitnow, Remotive. No personal data is transmitted.

        Delete your data

        Delete storage.json + clear LocalStorage (F12 → Application → LocalStorage).
